## English short description (draft)
Find homes, flats, shops and plots for rent or sale across Gujarat.

## English full description (draft)
Maru Ghar helps people discover property listings and submit their own properties for rent or sale across Gujarat.

Browse homes, flats, shops, offices, residential and commercial plots, warehouses and PG listings. Search by city and area, review available property details, and contact owners through the contact options available on approved listings.

Property owners can submit listing details with photos or videos. Submissions are reviewed by the administrator before they are shown publicly. Customers can create an account to use favorites and enquiry-related features. The app supports Gujarati, Hindi and English.

Maru Ghar does not publicly display an owner's private phone number on a property card. Please read the Privacy Policy for details about information collection, storage, account deletion and contact options.
